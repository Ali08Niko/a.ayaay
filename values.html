MY GUIDING PRINCIPLES

How My Values Guide Me

When Making Decisions

I try to think about the possible outcomes of my decisions instead of acting without thinking, how it would effect others and what would happen if I made a different decision. I want to make choices that I can stand by and that reflect what I believe is right.

When Building Relationships

I believe that good relationships are built through trust, respect, honesty, and understanding. I want to surround myself with people who encourage growth and make me feel comfortable being myself.

In My Everyday Actions

I try to show my values through the little things I do, such as keeping my promises, being considerate, helping others, and taking responsibility for my tasks.

MY PERSONAL PHILOSOPHY

What I Believe About Life

I believe that life is a continuous process of learning and becoming a better person. I don't think I need to have everything figured out right away because everyone has their own pace and journey.

What matters to me is making an effort, learning from my experiences, and staying true to the values that are important to me. I want to grow without losing the qualities that make me who I am.

A REMINDER TO MYSELF

The Person I Choose to Be

"I may not always make the perfect decisions, but I can always choose to learn from them. I want my actions to reflect the values I believe in and the person I am working toward becoming."
